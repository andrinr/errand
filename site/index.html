03 / The playable benchmark suite

5 GAMES. QUESTIONABLE WIN CONDITIONS.
▤   README.txt — Notepad×

Did the thing actually get done?

Five interactive environments test your ability to do one ordinary thing while the interface actively resists you. Cursed office mode adds tighter timers, interruptions, and interface traps. Practice gives you more time to discover the solution. Every game has a completion path.

01. Give it an errand.

One clear task. A messy environment. No bonus points for explaining why it’s hard.

02. Look for the outcome.

The page came out. The membership ended. The slide appeared. “Done!” is not evidence.

03. Count the collateral.

Time wasted, accounts created, unnecessary subscriptions, paper, ink, power, and caffeine. All consumed resources count toward the total cost.

This is a parody and a prototype, not a scientific evaluation. The leaderboard is hand-authored fiction. The games are browser simulations, with no real-world actions. Session wins are real local gameplay; leaderboard scores are fiction. This is an unaffiliated parody inspired by ARC-AGI-3, not an official ARC benchmark. Real model evaluation is future work.
